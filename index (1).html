Process

Approach

I trade equities long and short from a discretionary, top-down perspective. Every trade begins with a regime assessment — reading the tape at the index level, filtering into the strongest sectors, then identifying individual names with the best risk-reward and structural setup.

Regime Assessment

Market breadth, gamma positioning, and index-level price action establish the directional bias and risk appetite before any single-name work begins.

Sector Rotation

Relative strength across sectors and industry groups narrows the universe. I focus capital where institutional flow is concentrated.

Name Selection

Relative volume, breakout structure, and fundamental catalysts drive individual stock selection. Confluence across timeframes is required.

Risk Management

Every position is sized to a defined risk budget. Staggered stops, systematic trimming on divergences, and clear invalidation levels govern every trade.
